# Why this version succeeded

## It was not one template

The composition combines custom scene-specific layouts with reusable principles. The cards, timelines, strike-through tools, B-axis modules, depth reveal, and finale were authored for their phrases. Reuse their semantic pattern, not their coordinates.

## Key improvements over weak drafts

- The source footage stayed visible instead of being buried under a full-frame dark or milky treatment.
- Effects became larger and were allowed to cover the face when they were the message.
- Copy followed phrase-level audio timing rather than broad scene timing.
- B-axis switching was concentrated in the showcase sentence instead of repeated throughout.
- Empty geometric components gained labels, icons, diagrams, and motion meaning.
- Multi-clause sentences accumulated before exiting as a group.
- The legacy-tool section fused wording and strike action into one visual event.
- Finale lights and confetti remained active through the final frame.

## What to preserve

- clean A-roll background;
- large 得意黑-style Chinese display typography;
- warm neutral surfaces with blue, violet, coral, green, and yellow accents;
- broad low-contrast shadows;
- editorial card geometry;
- one seek-safe GSAP timeline;
- exact phrase synchronization;
- semantic variation between consecutive animation scenes.

## What must change per video

- phrase grouping;
- scene hierarchy;
- component content;
- recipe selection;
- color emphasis;
- A/B-axis allocation;
- timing and density.
