# Visual breakdown

## Why this reference works

The TraeWork version separates responsibilities cleanly: the caption layer carries the complete spoken wording, the talking head carries presence and delivery, and MG answers one concrete viewer question at a time. Real screenshots remain evidence rather than decorative cards.

## Reusable decisions

- **Opening proof:** show the supplied proof image at its original ratio and readable scale. Do not invent a statistic label over it when the source image already contains the proof.
- **Brand identification:** place the supplied logo or product evidence on the phrase that names the product. Keep the brand spelling exactly as supplied.
- **Relationship before evidence:** when speech explains a tool-to-model relationship, introduce the compact relationship diagram, then replace it with the supporting screenshot. The screenshot must remain complete and legible.
- **Search-result focus:** keep the search screenshot at one scale. Use synchronized top and bottom masks or external shading to focus the CN result, the international result, and the recommended CN result without zooming or horizontal cropping.
- **GitHub controls:** show the repository and terminal evidence as separate complete frames. Use external shading to focus the real HTTPS/copy or Download ZIP controls; never cover command-line content with a decorative label.
- **Ordered information:** use a vertical build for workflows and other dependent lists. Reveal each item at the spoken cue, keep earlier items visible, and exit the group together.
- **Input to output:** for finance or other process explanations, make the causal direction explicit: evidence/input on top, result/task below. Avoid four parallel labels when the speech describes a progression.
- **Product capability:** let the evidence image and the AI-video result share the same semantic scene, then exit them together. Do not add an unrelated image-to-video claim.
- **Demonstration stage:** use one continuous B-axis range for a dense hands-on recording. Keep the live speaker in a moving circular window at the lower-left and retain the captions; do not add unrelated MG inside the reserved stage.

## Spatial and timing guardrails

- One primary flow per MG group: horizontal for a replacement chain, vertical for a hierarchy or workflow.
- A-axis groups replace rather than accumulate. B-axis elements may accumulate only within one coherent page and leave together.
- Start the first meaningful event on the spoken anchor and keep the resolved state visible long enough to inspect.
- If evidence does not fit, shorten the shot or switch sooner. Do not shrink, distort, or crop the source to preserve a long hold.
- Keep captions in the lower safe zone and keep evidence, MG, and the protected B-axis PiP out of collision.
